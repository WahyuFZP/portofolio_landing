## Problem

The Madiun City Environmental Agency (DLH) collected waste-service retribution manually, door to door. That process is slow, hard to audit, and makes it difficult to track who has paid.

## Solution

A web-based E-Retribution system built with Laravel and MySQL, integrated with a payment gateway. Residents pay digitally, and the agency gets an automated record of every payment.

## Impact

- Replaces manual door-to-door collection with an automated digital flow.
- Improves transparency: every payment is recorded in the system.
- Simplifies revenue management for public waste services.
